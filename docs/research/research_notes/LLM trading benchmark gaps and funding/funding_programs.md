# Research compute and grant programs for an independent open-source LLM-evaluation researcher (as of 2026-10-13)

Scope: compute and cash programs an individual with no company and no university affiliation could use for LLM evaluation benchmark work. These are eligibility facts only, not legal or immigration advice. "Not verified" means I did not confirm the claim on an official page in this session. Research was capped at about 19 tool calls, so several programs rest on secondary sources. They are flagged.

## Which programs accept individuals with no company and no university affiliation?

### Takeaway
The best options that are open to unaffiliated individuals are Thinking Machines' Tinker research grants (credits from $5k, reviewed on a rolling basis, about 1 week), Anthropic's External Researcher Access Program (API credits for safety/alignment work, reviewed monthly), Google TRC (free TPUs, not limited to academia), Modal academic credits (up to $10k, tied to a conference submission), Hugging Face ZeroGPU community grants (for demo Spaces), and cash funders Emergent Ventures, LTFF, Manifund and Coefficient Giving. OpenAI RAP, AWS/Google research credits, NVIDIA Academic Grants and Anthropic AI for Science all require an academic or research-organization affiliation. NSF effectively excludes unaffiliated individuals.

### Inferences
- An unaffiliated LLM-evals researcher fits these best, in order: (1) Anthropic ERAP, if the benchmark is framed as safety or alignment evaluation; (2) Tinker research grant, if the benchmark involves fine-tuning open-weight models; (3) TRC, if the code is JAX/PyTorch-XLA (it is less useful for API-model evals); (4) Modal credits, if there is a target conference submission; (5) HF ZeroGPU, for hosting a public leaderboard or demo Space.
- OpenAI RAP technically allows "other research organization" affiliation. A nonprofit or fiscal-sponsor affiliation might qualify. Not verified.
- For an LLM-evaluation benchmark, API credits (Anthropic, OpenAI, Tinker) are worth more than raw GPU credits, because most eval spend goes to model inference calls.

## Which provide cash vs only credits?

### Takeaway
Cash comes only from philanthropic and government funders: Coefficient Giving, LTFF, Manifund, Emergent Ventures, Schmidt Sciences, NSF, and Thinking Machines' Interactivity grants (now closed). Every company program (Thinking Machines Tinker, Lambda, Google, AWS, NVIDIA, Microsoft, OpenAI, Anthropic, Modal, Together, HF, Cerebras) gives credits, compute or hardware only.

### Cited Findings
- Tinker grants are credits ("free credits"). Interactivity grants were $100k plus $25k credits (not verified) — [Thinking Machines](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/tinker-research-and-teaching-grants/), [grantedai](https://grantedai.com/grants/thinking-machines-lab-interactivity-research-grants-real-time-multimodal-ai-tml-d9f3a6c2)
- NVIDIA Inception: no cash grants mentioned. Benefits are partner credits, training and hardware rebates — [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/)
- NVIDIA Academic Grant gives GPU-hours or hardware — [grantedai listing](https://grantedai.com/grants/nvidia-academic-grant-program-for-researchers-nvidia-e85f94e5)
- Google credits "do not have commercial value" — [Google for Education](https://edu.google.com/programs/credits/research/)
- Manifund 2025 regrantors each had $100k+ to give as grants — [Manifund](https://manifund.substack.com/p/manifund-2025-regrants)
- Coefficient Giving TAIS RFP expects about $40M in grants (secondary) — [grantedai](https://grantedai.com/grants/coefficient-giving-rfp-technical-ai-safety-research-alignment-interpretability-ai-control)
- Schmidt Sciences AI2050 and the multi-agent safety call are cash awards (aggregator) — [grantedai](https://grantedai.com/blog/schmidt-sciences-scaling-ai-safety-multi-agent-1-million-august-8-deadline-private-philanthropy-research-strategy-2026)

### Inferences
- For a researcher who must remain non-commercial, credits avoid revenue or payment questions. Cash grants paid to an individual may carry tax or other status implications that the owner should check with a professional. That is outside the scope of these notes.

### Gaps
- Emergent Ventures does not publish typical amounts on its official page.
- LTFF typical grant sizes for individuals are not verified.

## Which require incorporating a startup?

### Takeaway
NVIDIA Inception, AWS Activate, Google for Startups Cloud and Microsoft for Startups all require a startup. Inception explicitly requires incorporation. Activate requires a company website or profile and a founding date. The Google AI tier requires VC funding. Microsoft has a track for unfunded startups. None of the research, API-credit or philanthropic programs require a company.

### Cited Findings
- NVIDIA Inception requires an officially incorporated company with an incorporation date, at least 1 developer, a working website, and age under 10 years — [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/)
- AWS Activate requires pre-Series B, a company website or profile, founding within 10 years, and an AWS paid-tier account — [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/activate/credits)
- Google for Startups AI tier requires VC funding from seed to Series A and founding within 10 years — [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/startup/ai)
- Microsoft for Startups added an unfunded-startup track (up to $5k Azure) from 2025-07-02 — [Dutch IT Channel](https://www.dutchitchannel.nl/news/660443/wijzigingen-in-het-microsoft-for-startups-programma)
- Academic programs need institutional affiliation instead: AWS research credits (faculty, staff or students) — [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/research-credits); Google research credits — [Google](https://edu.google.com/programs/credits/research/); NVIDIA Academic Grant (faculty) — [NVIDIA](https://nvidia.com/en-us/industries/higher-education-research/academic-grant-program/); OpenAI RAP — [OpenAI FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10139500-researcher-access-program-faq)

### Inferences
- Startup programs are a poor fit for someone deliberately staying non-commercial. Their eligibility presumes a company with a product, and Google's AI tier presumes VC funding.

### Gaps
- Whether Microsoft's unfunded track requires a registered legal entity, or only a self-declared startup, was not verified on an official Microsoft page.
